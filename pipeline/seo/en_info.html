Israel Drone Map – where can I fly a drone in Israel?

A free map for drone and UAS pilots in Israel. Tap any point on the map, or "Check here", to see what applies there: prohibited, restricted and danger areas, UAS flight areas, airports, airstrips and heliports, and temporary closures from NOTAMs.

This is an aid only. The binding sources are the official Israeli AIP and NOTAMs published by the Civil Aviation Authority of Israel. Check them before every flight.

Where can I fly a drone in Israel?

It depends on the place, the height and the time. The map combines the areas from the Israeli AIP with temporary NOTAM closures, and shows the limits and the contacts for coordination at every point.

What is a NOTAM?

An official notice about a temporary change in the airspace, such as a closed area, special activity or a new obstacle. The map reads the NOTAMs every 6 hours and draws them. When the location or size is unclear, it shows an approximate area in a distinct color together with the original NOTAM text.

What is the AIP?

The Aeronautical Information Publication of Israel (in Hebrew: פמ"ת). It is the source of the flight areas, no-fly zones, airports and airstrips. The map checks it daily and shows when it was last updated.

What else is on the map?

Point weather (wind, gusts, wind at 100 m, rain, visibility, cloud base, sunrise and sunset), ground elevation, obstacles, built-up areas and crowds, low-level air traffic, and "Who to call?" for coordination.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up and no install, and it works on a phone.
